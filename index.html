Apsara

In Indian tradition, an Apsara is a celestial figure of beauty and grace. We chose the name as a promise — every woman who sits in our chair should leave feeling exactly that: graceful, cared for, herself.

Our Story

A parlour built on trust, not trends

Apsara Professionals began as one woman's craft and has grown into Jagadhri's trusted name for ladies-only beauty care. What hasn't changed is the promise Rajni Sharma made when she started: every client is treated with patience, respect and complete privacy.

Today, a team of trained and experienced staff works alongside her, each one taught the same standard — skilled hands, honest advice, and a calm, welcoming room for women of every age.

Meet the Founder

Rajni Sharma

Founder & Chief Beautician, Apsara Professionals

"Every woman who walks in should walk out feeling seen, cared for, and beautiful in her own way."

With over 25 years in the beauty industry, Rajni Sharma has built Apsara Professionals into a name Jagadhri families rely on for makeup, skincare and grooming. Her approach blends technical skill with genuine warmth — she trains every member of her staff personally, insisting on the same care and courtesy she'd want for her own family.

From everyday grooming to bridal makeup, Rajni ma'am and her team bring decades of hands-on experience to every appointment, in a space where comfort and privacy always come first.
